How It Works

The renovation roadmap, from first meeting to approval

Our process, as outlined on our site.

1. Tell us about it

Share the project, the goals and your timing.

2. Design meeting

We discuss scope and goals, set a timeline, develop a preliminary budget, and measure and photograph your spaces.

3. Design presentation

A Zoom presentation with elevations, renderings and concept schematic drawings, then a team and subcontractor review.

4. Budget and approval

Choose materials at showrooms, review the revised cost, and approve the final design, drawings and price.
